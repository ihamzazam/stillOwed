About StillOwed

Born at a hackathon. Aimed at a drawer full of invoices one of us grew up watching.

WHERE THIS STARTED

StillOwed started as a hackathon project, and technically it still is one: we are building it right now, in public, during a Google hackathon. The product is new. The problem is not.

Hamza grew up around his father's business, watching the same scene repeat: work delivered, invoice sent, client gone quiet. Some invoices got paid after a reminder or two. Plenty did not, and chasing them was its own kind of work: awkward, repetitive, and always less urgent than the next paying job. Past a certain age, those invoices stopped being money and became furniture.

So when the hackathon came around, Hamza went deep on the problem: how much small-firm revenue dies this way, why collection agencies usually make it worse, and why so many of these invoices were never actually refused, just lost in someone's accounts payable pile. Then he teamed up with Danish to build the thing the family business never had.

An agent that never forgets, never threatens, and never gets tired of asking nicely.

WHY IT MATTERS

His father's drawer is not unique. Every small service firm has one: invoices sixty, ninety, a hundred and twenty days old that were never disputed, just postponed into oblivion. The traditional answer is a collection agency, and for most small firms the cure is worse than the loss: aggressive letters from a stranger, a burned client relationship, and the queasy feeling of having sent someone you once worked with "to collections."

People are bad at the polite, persistent nudge these invoices actually need. It is awkward, repetitive, and easy to postpone. Software is excellent at it.

THE RULES WE BUILD BY

An AI that chases money on your behalf should be boring, careful, and accountable. Ours operates inside rules that are enforced in code and published for anyone to read:

✓It speaks in your name, in your voice. Your customer never meets a collection agency.
✓It works B2B debt only, never consumers, and never sends a threat of any kind.
✓It negotiates only inside hard limits you set. Anything unusual goes to a human, instantly.
✓A dispute freezes everything in seconds. The AI never argues. Sometimes your customer is right.
✓Money goes straight from your customer to you. We are structurally incapable of holding it.
✓We earn 20% of what lands, $0 otherwise. If you recover nothing, you owe nothing.

The full list lives in our public Guardrail Register. When a rule and a recovery conflict, the rule wins and the recovery waits.
